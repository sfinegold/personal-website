Chapter IV

The Pensieve

Ten questions, no wrong answers. Take them slowly, one or two over coffee. They save to this device and nowhere else.

I

It's January 2nd and the trip worked. What's true now that wasn't in August?

One sentence. This is the spell the whole map serves.

II

What's the loaf?

By New Year's you can bake one thing well enough to give it away. Name it now.

III

Who are the seven hugs?

The people you must see before January. The map bends around these names.

IV

What would make these months feel wasted?

Write the failure down so you can see it coming.

V

Describe one perfect, ordinary Tuesday in late November.

Not the highlight reel — the coffee, the walk, the work. Ordinary days are what Brooklyn and San Francisco are really offering.

VI

What will you do alone that scares you a little?

A night train. A solo dinner. A cold email to a famous baker.

VII

What will you leave behind on purpose?

A habit, a worry, a routine that ended with the clerkship.

VIII

What does 75-year-old Charlotte order you not to skip?

She's very wise, and slightly annoyed you had to ask.

IX

What's the souvenir that isn't a thing?

A skill, a friendship, a recipe, a decision.

X

Two weeks in a real bakery is a test of something. Of what?

Say it plainly, and December will answer it plainly back.
