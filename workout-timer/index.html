Rotator-cuff / impingement — right shoulder. Pain lives in the overhead arc, worst at ball impact. Play by the traffic light: 🟢 dull & settles = OK · 🟡 achy but gone next day = cap volume · 🔴 sharp catch / sore next morning = stop. Groundstrokes are usually fine — modulate the serve (50–60%, slice over kick, no smashes).
