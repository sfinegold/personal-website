01Where the stake fits — the 2×2

Two axes that decide the stake's effect on a role: supply (how crowded the pipeline is) and payout/skill (how big the bounty, how senior the role). The stake isn't a blanket tax — it's a precision tool that belongs in one quadrant and should be waived in others.

What it tells you: price by congestion, waive by scarcity, keep it small where payouts are low. The stake earns its keep in the top-right (crowded + lucrative senior roles) — which is exactly where the "crazed dogs" complaint lives — and should be dialed down or off everywhere supply is thin. This is the role-level tuning iteration, and it maps to the prototype's "stake waived · low supply" chip.

Use it to disarm two objections: "you're supply-constrained" → the whole left column waives. "Some HMs want the firehose" → bottom-right waives. You're not proposing a marketplace tax; you're proposing a scalpel.

02The spine + how you got here

One line: A bad submission costs a recruiter nothing, and with few submissions each you can't tell good from bad fast enough to filter — so HMs do the filtering. A refundable stake prices each submission at n=1: the one early signal nothing else gives you.

How I got here (7 beats — narrate in 90 sec):

  • The three HM quotes are one story — quality is the symptom.
  • Quality is a portfolio problem; pick one high-leverage bet I can ship fast.
  • Root cause is economic: bad submissions are free; the platform's filter needs 15–20 subs + weeks to converge, so HM attention absorbs the risk.
  • Mapped the option space (intake / checks / flow / incentives / demand); built two prototypes.
  • Chose the stake: prices the free externality, elicits the recruiter's private read, ships without ML, revenue-neutral.
  • Designed hard for fairness — because "cost to submit" is a backlash risk.
  • Got honest about the hard part: knowing if it worked is a real measurement problem.

03Why the stake over the dozen other levers

  • Everything Paraform already does is lagging & reputational — interview-rate floors, ratings, Preferred, streaks — all need 15–20 submissions to mean anything. The stake is the only lever that prices a single submission on day one, the exact new-recruiter window the existing tools can't cover. Complementary, not redundant.
  • Simple & messageable — complexity itself suppresses supply, so the fancier fixes (Signal-score tiers, AI-review pipeline) cost supply you can't spare.
  • Uses private info + keeps agency — the recruiter talked to the candidate; the stake lets them bet on their own read (override the algorithm with money).
  • Tunable — a dial (per tier, per role) to hit a target rejection rate, not a rebuild.
  • I'd retire the volume floors in the same move — you can't price an obligation, and the floor manufactures the forced junk. Net complexity-neutral, and removing it is part of the fix.

04Tough questions → your trigger

  • "Why the stake, not X?" → lagging-vs-forward-priced; it's the only per-submission, day-one signal.
  • "Won't it shrink supply?" (the one) → yes, deliberately, for junk. 3 losses: junk (good) / underconfident-good (keep it small) / cash-tight-skilled (fairness). Small stake (zero-price effect: signal comes from crossing zero, not the size). Iterations point loss back at growth.
  • "How would you know it worked?" → rate and count together; interference means the lift is a lower bound.
  • "Why new recruiters first?" → clean experiment + rules-not-takebacks + risk concentrates there.
  • "Don't we already do a lot for quality?" → yes, but all of it is reputation that needs a sample. The stake covers day one.
  • "Why might it fail?" → sentiment ("pay to work" screenshot) + slow/fuzzy measurement → start small, contained, reversible. (Have the two failure stories ready — adverse selection of the best; and mixed-results → dial it down / role-waive.)

05Measurement — the crux (go deep)

  • Power: +10pp off ~40% ≈ 385 subs/arm ≈ ~110 recruiters/arm (~220 total). Detectable effect = volume × duration. Pre-register the min effect; else before/after cohorts + interviews.
  • Interference: treated holds back → control's field thins → control flattered → bias is down → a clean win is a strong win. Magnitude ≈ treated-share × volume-cut (small for new recruiters). Query: treated share of subs per role; if a tail is big, partition the marketplace.
  • Rate vs. count: rate↑ can be denominator math (that's the mechanism) or a starving pipeline. Watch both. Fulfillment guardrail = final-rounders/recruiter, low-frequency & fuzzy → HM willingness-to-pay sets the tolerable dip, not the experiment.

06Hard numbers — don't blank

  • Preferred: score ≥2.75/4 · first-round ≥60% · mid-round ≥30% · monthly. Perks: priority review, early access, unlimited approvals, badge.
  • Interview rewards: $250 final-round · $500 offer-round · 1/candidate · paid by Paraform. Hire reward ~22.5% first-yr.
  • Enforcement: interview rate <30% flag → 25–30% approvals revoked (single-sub-only, 2-mo review) → ≤25% deactivation. Avg rating ≥2.5; a single 1/4 disables submissions.
  • Your stake: $50 Standard · $25 Preferred. Refund at screen pass. Forfeit → role pool, returned if role dies / 6mo.
  • Signal gap: new default rating 5.0; platform avg ~4.7. Live evidence: "please ease off" HM banner; pending-submission-limit card already exists.
