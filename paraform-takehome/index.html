The submission stake, end to end

A stake is a small refundable deposit a new recruiter places with each submission. This walkthrough follows one recruiter from signup through every way the money can move — refunded, charged, or released — and out the other side as her record earns the stake down to zero.

Design principles for the stake

  1. A price, not a punishment. The stake gates on confidence; it never blocks anyone from working.
  2. Refund on what the recruiter controls. Judged only on resume review and the screening call — not deep-funnel outcomes they can't steer.
  3. Paraform never profits from a bad submission. Forfeits fund the role's recruiter pool and return to recruiters if the role dies. Paraform earns only on a hire.
  4. Invisible to hiring managers. They never see amounts, so reviews stay about the candidate.
  5. Loud payout, quiet stake. The reward is the carrot and stays prominent; the stake is a mechanic — discreet, explained on demand.
