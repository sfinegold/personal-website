Problem Solution Impact
The problem

Volunteer capacity left on the table

  • Volunteers can only canvass when an organizer has cut turf for them — so huge volunteer walking capacity goes unused.
  • MiniVAN is clunky: a separate app that drains battery, takes lots of taps to mark a door, and isn't personalized to the campaign — or fun.
  • The result: people who want to help can't just pick up their phone and go.

Where it goes

From walking turf to a volunteer engine

Independent lit drop→ Organized events→ Canvassing→ Gamification→ Digital tasks
  • Deeper NGP integration: the API lets us read/write voters programmatically — e.g. spin up phone banking on the same base.
  • Relational organizing is the big unlock to fine-tune: nudge volunteers to bring in their friends & networks — the highest-trust, highest-converting outreach there is.
  • Because it's our own app, capabilities grow in near real-time — not limited to what VAN allows.

The bet: meet volunteers where they are, make helping feel good, and compound it through their networks.
